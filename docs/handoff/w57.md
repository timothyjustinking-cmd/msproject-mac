(The brief's `-DCMAKE_CXX_FLAGS=/fsanitize=address` drops `/EHsc` and fails with
C4530 under `/WX`; see F2.)

**F1 status.** Release is **fixed** by `core::formula::kMaxFormulaDepth = 160`
(positioned error; `--deep-guard 256` and `1024` PASS). The residual is that 160
levels exceed what an MSVC ASan Debug stack survives (64/72 parse; 80–160
crash), so `formula_tests` SEGFAULTs and the two guard tests fail in the ASan
tree. Repro:
`build\vs-w57-asan\tests\fuzz\Debug\fuzz_parsers.exe --deep-guard 160 --cases 1`.
Suggested fix: lower the constant to 64 or make the parser iterative; the guard
tests are enabled and must not be weakened to make the ASan gate green.
